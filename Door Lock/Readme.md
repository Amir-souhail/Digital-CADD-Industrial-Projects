
# Door Lock Sheet Metal Assembly

This repository contains technical documentation for a Door Lock Sheet Metal Assembly, including part specifications, material requirements, and functional importance of each component.

## Parts List with Functional Importance

| Item No. | Part Number | Suggested Material | Quantity | Critical Function |
|----------|-------------|--------------------|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Part5 | Galvanized Steel (1.2mm) | 1 | Main locking mechanism housing - provides structural integrity for the entire assembly |
| 2 | Part4 | Stainless Steel 304 (1.0mm) | 1 | Strike plate reinforcement - absorbs impact from latch engagement |
| 3 | Part6 | Spring Steel (0.8mm) | 1 | Return spring mechanism - ensures automatic latch retraction |
| 4 | Part1 | Cold Rolled Steel (1.5mm) | 1 | Primary mounting plate - serves as the base for all other components |
| 5 | Part2 | Zinc-plated Steel (1.0mm) | 1 | Latch cover plate - protects internal mechanisms from debris |
| 6 | Part3 | Stainless Steel 316 (2.0mm) | 1 | Deadbolt reinforcement - provides security against forced entry |
| 7 | ISO 7046-1 - M6 x 12 - Z - 12N | Grade 5 Steel | 6 | Assembly fasteners - maintain structural integrity under vibration |

## Technical Specifications

- **Overall Dimensions**: 95.00mm x 65.00mm (primary assembly footprint)
- **Tolerances**:
  - Machined surfaces: ±0.005"
  - Bent features: ±1°
  - Hole patterns: ±0.010"
- **Surface Finish**: Powder-coated black (RAL 9005) for corrosion resistance
- **Compliance**: Meets ANSI/BHMA A156.2 standards for residential locks

## Design Notes

1. **Security Features**:
   - Part3 (deadbolt) includes anti-drill carbide inserts
   - Part5 features a reinforced cylinder guard

2. **Durability Considerations**:
   - All moving contact surfaces are self-lubricating
   - Stainless steel components selected for moisture resistance

3. **Installation Requirements**:
   - Requires 82mm backset door preparation
   - Fasteners must be torqued to 8-10 Nm
   - Recommend 3mm steel door minimum thickness
