# 🔩 Belt and Pulley Assembly – SolidWorks Project

This repository contains the 3D CAD models and technical drawings for a Belt and Pulley Assembly created using **SolidWorks 2025**. The assembly demonstrates mechanical power transmission between a DC motor and a driven shaft using a belt and pulley mechanism. It serves as an educational and demonstrative model for mechanical design, kinematics, and CAD documentation.

![Belt and Pulley Assembly](./Belt%20and%20Pulley%20Assembly.JPG)

---

## 🧠 Project Objective

To design, simulate, and document a **belt-driven mechanical power transmission system**, which includes:

- A base-mounted **DC motor**
- **Driver and driven pulleys**
- **Elastic belt**
- **Supporting structure** with shaft holders and fasteners

The model highlights how rotational motion is transferred from the motor shaft to another shaft using friction and tension in the belt.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

A **DC motor** drives the smaller pulley, which is connected via a **belt** to a larger driven pulley mounted on a shaft. The **belt transmits power** through friction, converting the motor’s rotation into shaft motion.

- The **motor pulley** is the driver.
- The **shaft pulley** is the driven unit.
- The **belt** must maintain proper tension for efficient power transfer.
- **Support brackets** hold the shaft securely to the wooden base.
