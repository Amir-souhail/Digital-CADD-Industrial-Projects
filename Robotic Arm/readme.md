# Robotic Arm Assembly T130

This project presents a fully modeled and assembled **Robotic Arm with Gripper**, designed in SOLIDWORKS. The robotic arm is actuated using servo motors and includes multiple articulated links, pivot joints, and a precision gripper (Robotic Gripper 2). The design demonstrates modularity, real-world fastening practices, and dynamic actuation simulation.

---

## 🔧 Overview

The robotic arm is ideal for object handling, automation, or educational platforms. It includes 37 parts, including servo mounts, structural links, ISO standard fasteners, and a pre-integrated gripper assembly. Three servo motors provide the main actuation of the arm.

## 🔩 Fasteners & Standards

The design uses ISO-standard mechanical fasteners, including:
- Screws: M1.6, M2, M3, M4 in various lengths
- Nuts: ISO 4032, ISO 4035, ISO 4036
- Dowel Pins: ISO 13337
- Spring Pins: ISO 8752
- Shoulder Pins: ISO 8733

All fasteners are included in the 3D model and BOM for realistic assembly constraints.

## 🧱 Material Suggestions

To ensure functionality, cost-efficiency, and durability, here are suggested materials:

| Part Type             | Recommended Material    | Justification |
|-----------------------|-------------------------|---------------|
| Structural Arms       | **Aluminum 6061-T6**     | Lightweight, strong, easy to machine |
| Servo Brackets        | **ABS Plastic or Nylon** | Impact-resistant and servo-safe |
| Pins and Shafts       | **Stainless Steel (AISI 304)** | Corrosion-resistant, high strength |
| Gripper Parts         | **PETG / Nylon**         | High durability and moderate flexibility |
| Fasteners             | **Zinc-plated Steel**    | Readily available, strong, corrosion protected |
