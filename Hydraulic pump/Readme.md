
# Hydraulic Pump Assembly

This project presents the complete 3D CAD model and exploded view of a **Hydraulic Gear Pump**. The design includes a full bill of materials (BOM), proper assembly modeling, and realistic part interactions. Developed using SolidWorks 2025, this model demonstrates the internal gear engagement and structural components essential for low-pressure hydraulic applications.

## 🛠️ Assembly Overview

The assembly simulates a typical **external gear pump**, where:
- The **drive shaft gear** transmits torque into the system,
- The **idle gear** rotates by meshing with the drive gear,
- **Oil is pulled** from the inlet side and **forced out** through the outlet by gear rotation,
- **Seals and plates** ensure oil-tight enclosures under pressure.
