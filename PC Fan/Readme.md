# PC Fan Assembly

This project involves the mechanical CAD modeling and documentation of a standard PC cooling fan using SOLIDWORKS. The assembly includes all major components required to simulate a functional axial fan, commonly used in computer hardware for thermal management.

## 🔧 Main Function

The **PC Fan Assembly** is designed to cool internal components of a computer by drawing ambient air through its rotating impeller and pushing it across electronic devices, such as processors or power supplies. The fan is powered by a DC motor and secured using standardized fasteners.

## 🧩 Bill of Materials and Component Roles

| Item No | Part Number        | Description                   | Qty | Function |
|---------|--------------------|-------------------------------|-----|----------|
| 1       | PART1_PC           | Fan Frame                     | 1   | Holds all components and mounts the fan to the chassis |
| 2       | PART3_PC           | Motor Housing/Cap             | 1   | Encases and supports the motor or shaft region |
| 3       | PART4_PC WIRES     | Electrical Wires              | 1   | Delivers power from the source to the DC motor |
| 4       | PART10_DCM         | DC Motor                      | 1   | Drives the impeller to generate airflow |
| 5       | PART2_PC           | Impeller (Fan Blades)         | 1   | Rotates to move air, creating forced convection |
| 6       | PART5              | Shaft                         | 1   | Connects motor to the impeller, transmits torque |
| 7       | ISO 1224 - 170204  | Standard Fasteners            | 1   | Used to assemble and secure the components |
