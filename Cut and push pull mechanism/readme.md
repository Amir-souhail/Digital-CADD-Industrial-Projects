
# Cut and Push-Pull Mechanism

This project demonstrates a mechanical system designed to perform a combined cutting and push-pull motion using a Geneva wheel and gear-based mechanism. The project was designed and assembled in SOLIDWORKS and includes the key mechanical components, a DC motor, and standardized gears for motion transfer.

---

## 🔧 Assembly Overview

The mechanism features a DC motor-driven Geneva mechanism that translates continuous rotary motion into intermittent linear movement. The design is commonly used in industrial automation systems where periodic linear motion is required.

## ⚙️ Mechanism Functionality

1. The **DC motor** rotates continuously, driving the **15T spur gear**.
2. The motion is transferred to the **22T gear**, which controls the **Geneva wheel**.
3. The **Geneva wheel** transforms continuous motion into intermittent rotation.
4. The **rack arm** attached to the wheel delivers a push-pull motion at precise intervals.
5. The **pushing arm** performs the desired mechanical work such as pressing, stamping, or shifting.
